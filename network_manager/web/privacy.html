Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026 · Version 2026-10-16

This policy describes the current Netwizzard application at netwizzard.tech. It covers account information, network-management records, authentication, VPN-check metadata and requests you send to the operator.

1. Operator and scope

Olamide Bello operates this deployment. For information submitted by your organization, your tenant administrator may also decide why and how that information is used. Contact your administrator about workplace records, or submit an operator request using Contact and requests. This policy does not describe an unrelated website or your VPN provider's practices.

2. Information processed

  • Accounts: name, email address, requested workspace name, role, approval/access status, creation time and, for new registrations under this version, the terms version and acceptance time.
  • Authentication: salted password hashes, hashed API keys and session tokens, session expiry and attempt-limit records. The application does not store your password in plaintext. A protected server bootstrap file may retain an initial administrator API key until the operator removes or replaces it.
  • Workspace records: devices and addresses, notes, ownership, group and topology data, telemetry, events, alerts, preferences, change requests, approvals, schedules, audit records, manually entered configuration drafts and uploaded scripts.
  • VPN sessions: user/tenant association, authentication-token hash, selected mode and zone, lease/deadline times, and device/port check audit metadata. VPN private keys are provisioned separately on the server, not collected by the browser form. The operator, VPN endpoint and destination may observe relevant connection metadata.
  • Requests and technical logs: the email, category and message you submit through the contact form; request and resolution times; and server/proxy logs that may include IP addresses, timestamps, requested URLs, browser information and errors. Authentication throttling uses a hash derived from the direct peer and relevant request information. Hosting and log configuration affect technical retention.

3. Purposes and access

We use this information to provide workspaces and sign-in, administer accounts, perform requested network checks and workflows, display monitoring results, investigate abuse and respond to requests. Tenant-scoped APIs restrict workspace records by role and tenant. Platform administrators can review registration information, tenant administration, platform audit, zone assignments and contact requests. Operators with server or database access can access stored information; API isolation does not prevent authorized server administration.

4. Browser storage and integrations

The current console keeps authentication and VPN-session tokens in tab memory, not browser local storage or authentication cookies. Closing a tab clears its in-memory copies; server-side sessions expire separately. Dashboard preferences are stored in the database. The application does not currently embed advertising trackers, third-party analytics, marketing cookies or a data-selling feature. Hosting/network providers still handle traffic. No external AI service receives your workspace data as part of the current application.

5. Sharing and service providers

Information is available to authorized workspace users, platform/server operators and hosting or network providers as necessary for operation. Requested device checks and enabled automation communicate with the selected target systems; platform VPN checks also use the configured VPN infrastructure. We may disclose relevant information where legally required or to investigate security abuse and protect rights, subject to applicable law. A new integration that changes these practices requires an updated disclosure.

6. Retention and security

Account, workspace, audit, request and backup records currently have no universal automatic deletion schedule; they remain until the operator removes them or an applicable retention obligation requires otherwise. Password sessions expire after eight hours; VPN leases expire after 90 seconds without renewal, with a one-hour maximum. Expired session and attempt records are cleaned during relevant API activity rather than immediately at every expiry time. Expiry ends access even when a database row remains.

The application uses password hashing, token hashing, access controls and HTTPS in the deployed reverse proxy. Backups and VPN-key files require server-side protection. These controls reduce risk but do not guarantee absolute security. Do not submit secrets in ordinary text fields. Signing out does not erase server records or backups.

7. Your choices and requests

You can change a password, replace a key, sign out, disconnect a VPN session and export supported workspace collections within your permissions. Use Contact and requests to ask for access, correction, deletion, account closure or other privacy rights available under applicable law. We may need proportionate identity/authority verification, and may retain information required by law or necessary for legitimate security purposes. Do not send identity documents through the request form unless a separate secure process is arranged. We do not promise automatic deletion or a statutory response period without knowing which law applies.

8. Location, age and changes

The hosting and VPN locations depend on the deployment and selected zone; processing may occur outside your country. This page does not claim any particular cross-border certification or transfer mechanism. The service is for adults 18 and older and is not directed to children. Contact us if a child's information was submitted. Updates show a new effective date/version, and required notices or choices will be provided for material changes.
